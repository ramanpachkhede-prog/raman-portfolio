Placement Compass

A college-placement decision helper built with Python, Streamlit and MySQL.

The problem

Students choosing a college for courses like BCA, BBA or B.Com usually compare placement claims scattered across brochures and websites, with no easy way to check them against their own marks and budget. Placement Compass puts placement rate, packages, fees, cut-offs and recruiters side by side, and ranks the colleges a student can realistically get into and afford.

What it does

Find colleges for me

Enter course, 12th marks, yearly budget, interest and city. Get the top 5 matches with a score and a plain-language reason.

Look up a college

One college and course: placement rate, average and highest package, recruiters, and year-by-year trend charts.

Compare colleges

Pick two or three colleges for the same course and see fees, cut-offs and outcomes in one table and chart.

How the match score works

Step 1, hard filters: only colleges offering the chosen course, with fees within budget and a cut-off at or below the student's marks (and the chosen city, if any).

Step 2, weighted score (0 to 100): each factor is min-max normalized across the remaining colleges, then combined:

Placement rate
40%
Average package
25%
Interest match
20%
Affordability
15%

Each result also gets a short explanation, for example "82% placed, avg ₹4.1 LPA, strong in Data Science, ₹12,000 under your budget".

Architecture

Demo CSVs or MySQL views→data_repository.py (load + validate)→recommender.py (filter + score)→app.py (Streamlit UI)
  • Separated layers: the UI, scoring logic and data access live in separate modules, so the data source can change without touching the pages.
  • Two data modes: runs on fictional demo data by default, and switches to a MySQL database with one environment variable.
  • Verified data only: the MySQL schema stores a source document and verification date for every figure, and the views expose only complete, verified records to the app.
  • Validation everywhere: the loader script checks URLs, numbers, dates and that placed students never exceed graduating students; the app re-validates data before showing it.
